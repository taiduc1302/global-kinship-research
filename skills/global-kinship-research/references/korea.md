# Korea research guidance (first pass)

Vocabulary: **족보** (jokbo; genealogy), **본관** (bon-gwan; ancestral clan seat), **항렬** (hangnyeol; generational naming), **호적** (household register). Keep Hangul and Hanja as separately sourced forms. Do not automatically generate Hanja from a Hangul name.

## Archive leads, manual verification required

- SKKU Korean Genealogy Database: https://jokbo.skku.edu/ENG/intro/introduction.jsp — lineage, clan and individuals in historical family registers.
- National Archives of Korea: https://www.archives.go.kr/ — searchable catalog; check restrictions on originals and copies.
- Digital Jangseogak: https://jsg.aks.ac.kr/ — historical textual/image sources; identify permitted reuse before downloading or publishing.

## Evidence concerns

- Similar surnames, generational syllables, or bon-gwan cannot prove a parent-child relationship.
- Traditional lunisolar dates, regnal naming and sexagenary signs need explicit source-context and a reviewed conversion method.
- Be careful of lineage books that omit or represent family members differently because of time-period and social conventions.
- Record the exact original page, edition, clan branch and source independence; multiple images of an edition are not independent witnesses.
