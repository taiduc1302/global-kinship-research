# Additional Asian regional research guidance (v0.2)

This is a directory of official **research leads**, not a dataset or crawler. Verify each current landing page, institutional record access, restrictions and reproduction rights. The Python profiles hold the complete active URLs and metadata.

## China — Jiāpu / Zúpǔ (家谱 / 族谱)

Family and clan histories; record original characters, lineage branch, edition and source page. A generation character or surname is not enough to identify an ancestor. [Shanghai Library Genealogy Library](https://www.library.sh.cn/special/GenealogyLibrary/) offers genealogy catalogs; collection indexes are not an automatic license for redistribution.

## Japan — Koseki and historical archives

Record Japanese-era dates and historical household relationships as written; avoid equating household membership with biological kinship. **Koseki** family registers have restricted access; a government historical archive is not an unrestricted koseki database. [National Archives of Japan](https://www.archives.go.jp/english/gettingstarted/access.html) describes its historical catalog and access process.

## Taiwan — family documents and colonial-era records

Original names can appear in Chinese and Japanese sources; preserve script and administrative context. Some collections include family papers and historical passports. [Academia Sinica Institute of Taiwan History](https://archives.ith.sinica.edu.tw/services_con_en.php?no=9) describes its archives and digital resources.

## Philippines — parish, civil and Spanish-period records

Check parish/province boundaries, record type, year and naming conventions before assuming identity. [National Archives of the Philippines research service](https://reference.nationalarchives.gov.ph/oprequest?page=landing-page) provides an official request route; [Erección de los Pueblos](https://erecciones.nationalarchives.gov.ph/oprequest) supports historical locality research.

## Thailand — national archives

Keep Thai script literal, and record whether a year is Buddhist Era or Gregorian. [National Archives of Thailand](https://www.nat.go.th/) provides catalog research guidance; online availability varies by record type.

## Indonesia — ANRI / JIKN

Colonial, island and local record systems differ. Preserve original language and context. [ANRI](https://www.anri.go.id/en/public-services/national-archival-information-system-and-network-services) documents regional JIKN and finding aids. Participating sources do not imply complete coverage.

## Malaysia — National Archives

Naming, language, script and calendars vary by community. [Online Finding Aids](https://ofa.arkib.gov.my/ofa/group/general/1) are searchable catalogs; check specific access restrictions.

## India — national archive references

Regional naming practices and calendars are diverse. [National Archives of India — Abhilekh Patal](https://www.nationalarchives.nic.in/en/online-portal-abhilekh-patal) is a government historical records portal, not a substitute for local vital-registration offices.

For each locale, report: literal record excerpt, script/language, event date and recorded calendar, historical place, custodial institution, precise page/record reference, source independence group, rights/access notes, unresolved contradictions. Only call a record 'found' if it was actually retrieved from a permitted source and independently inspected.
