# Ex-USSR historical research guidance

Use the upstream MIT-licensed ex-USSR Kinship Research Skill by Denis Shiryaev as a separate pinned reference: https://github.com/DenisSergeevitch/ex-ussr-kinship-research

Relevant historical periods include the Russian Empire, Soviet Union and independent successor states. Determine the actual administrative jurisdiction for **each event year** before identifying an archive. Preserve legal names, maiden names, patronymics, original script, place-name variants and date-calendar uncertainty. A translated index is a lead until confirmed against original records. Clearly track whether multiple websites mirror the same church-book scan, military file or civil record.

The upstream material is independently maintained. Do not imply that this skill incorporates all of its archive links or automatic functionality. Never copy materials from restricted digital archives merely because the instructions are MIT-licensed.
