# Vietnam research guidance (first pass)

Vocabulary: **gia phả**, **tộc phả** (genealogical registers), **họ**, **chi** (lineage branches), **đời** (generation), **giỗ** (death anniversary), **Hán-Nôm** (historical scripts). Retain the exact family-name / middle-name / given-name sequence; do not derive ethnicity or family relationship from a shared surname.

## Archive leads, manual verification required

- Institute of Hán-Nôm Studies: https://hannom.vass.gov.vn/ — heritage/manuscript catalogs. Some document images are not on the public site; ask about approved access rather than bypassing restrictions.
- National archives overview: https://history.state.gov/countries/archives/vietnam — useful orientation, but institution address/reader rules can change.

## Evidence concerns

- Record original Hán/Nôm characters *as seen* and record independent translation/transliteration, with a reviewer flag for uncertain glyphs.
- Preserve year/month/day of lunar entry and the specific calendar before any conversion; a lunar birth year alone is not a solar date.
- Historical villages and districts may have changed names, boundaries or archives. Search both historical and current names.
- Different names/titles for one person may be plausible, but never merge identities without cross-referenced relationships or distinct evidence.
- Family genealogies can be copied, redacted or retrospectively extended; list provenance and compare independent sources.
